How it works (for parents)

An equation is x wrapped in layers. To get x out, undo the outside layer first, and do the same thing to both sides. Every puzzle asks one question: what was the last thing done to x?

Escape peels random equations. Par is the fewest moves possible. Build lets him wrap his own and then escape it. Spot it makes him the marker. Bosses are real formulas from science.

Ten to fifteen minutes is plenty. Start each session with a Warm-up win. Progress is saved in this browser on this device only.
